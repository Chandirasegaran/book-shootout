      How to play

      Each player has five soldiers, numbered 1, 3, 5, 7 and 9. On your turn, open the book. Only the right-hand page counts, and it's always odd. Its last digit picks one of your soldiers. Page 127 means soldier 7.

      What happens to that soldier depends on how far it's drawn:

      1. Head
      2. Body
      3. Face
      4. Gun
      5. Bullet
      6. Shoot!
      • Shoot. When the page lands on a soldier that already has a bullet, it fires. Tap any enemy soldier to shoot it, even a half-drawn one.
      • A soldier only fires when the page lands on its number. After firing, the next landing reloads it.
      • A soldier that's shot out is gone. Landing on its number wastes your turn.

      Last player with a soldier standing wins.
